📖 Why order matters more than anything

1. The decision

Imagine you have decided it is time: your own company, your own brand and clients, a base in the Gulf.

2. The wall

Then the questions start. Which activity? Which legal form? Which documents, and do they need attestation or translation? Every source says something different, and one small mistake can send your application back and cost you weeks.

3. The realisation

The people who get through smoothly do not know more law. They do things in the right order, they prepare documents once and correctly, and someone tracks every step.

4. The 3 decisions

First, define your business activity, because it decides your licences. Second, choose your legal form with that activity in mind. Third, prepare your documents once, and check them before you submit.

5. The result

Imagine opening your inbox to see your registration issued, with your next steps already planned: bank account, bookkeeping, and your first customers.
