📝 How to Register a Company in Bahrain: A Step-by-Step Overview

By Ghazi Wazir · Updated October 3, 2026

1. Define your business activity

Start by writing down exactly what your company will sell and to whom. Your activity decides which licences and approvals you need, so it is the first decision to get right.

2. Choose a legal form

Bahrain offers several company types. Many activities allow foreign ownership, but some need extra approvals or a local partner, so check your activity before you decide.

3. Prepare your documents

Typical documents include passport copies for every owner, proof of address and any documents required for your activity. Some may need attestation or translation.

4. Apply for registration and licences

Your company is registered with the relevant authority and receives its commercial registration, then any activity-specific licences are issued.

5. Open a bank account and get set up

After registration, open a corporate bank account, set up bookkeeping and check your tax and VAT obligations.

Before you start

Rules, fees and timelines change and differ by activity. Confirm current requirements with the relevant authorities or a qualified adviser.

🛂 Investor Visa vs Worker Visa in Bahrain: What Is the Difference?

By Ghazi Wazir · Updated October 3, 2026

The main idea

An investor visa is linked to owning or investing in a company. A worker visa is linked to being employed by a company that sponsors you.

Who needs which

If you are a business owner or investor in a Bahrain company, the investor route is usually the one to look at. If you are hiring staff, each employee needs a work visa and residence permit.

What you will usually prepare

Both routes need passport copies, company documents and supporting papers. The exact list depends on your case and the authority.

Common mistakes

Missing or inconsistent documents are the most common cause of delays. A document review before submission saves time.

Next step

Eligibility and requirements are set by the relevant authorities and can change. A short consultation can confirm which route applies to you.
